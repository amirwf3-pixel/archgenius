## Known Limitations

- Polygon V1 orthogonal only, 3..8 verts, no self-intersection, no duplicate consecutive, no zero-length, area>=10, EPS 1e-6 — non-orthogonal rejected with explicit error, NOT_IMPLEMENTED for future
- L-shape notch corner mapping supports both short and long forms for UI compat, deterministic decomposition into 2 rects, for 8-vert more complex decomposition fallback to bounding rect but validation ensures rooms inside buildableBoundary polygon (not just bounding rect) via repair
- Setbacks are USER-DEFINED DESIGN INPUTS unless VERIFIED Tier-1, NOT legal enforcement, source/status preserved, no invented regs
- Parking fit is deterministic geometric, no overlap site/buildable/building/other stalls, perpendicular/parallel alternatives, but not full circulation simulation
- Out of scope: 3D/BIM/IFC/Revit/DWG/image-to-CAD/topography/terrain/neighbor sim/full solar/structural/MEP/municipality approval automation/unrestricted AI geometry/invented regs/FAR/coverage legal enforcement without Tier-1 — labeled NOT_IMPLEMENTED/advisory where applicable
